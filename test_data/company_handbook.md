# ExampleCo Employee Handbook

Effective date: 1 August 2026. Owner: People Operations. This is a fictional test document.

## Working together

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully, complete assigned training, and raise concerns early. A concern about conduct may be sent to a manager or to People Operations at `people@example.invalid`. A person may bypass their manager when the concern involves that manager. People Operations acknowledges a report within two working days and records the next step privately.

## Information handling

Use approved company accounts for work records. Do not paste employee payroll, medical, or disciplinary information into public chat tools. Report a lost device or a message sent to the wrong recipient to the security desk at `security@example.invalid` as soon as it is discovered. Access to an HR record is based on work need and the record's classification.

## Time away and pay

The [leave and attendance policy](leave_and_attendance_policy.md) describes requests, notice, and recording. Payslips are delivered to each employee's private HR portal inbox after payroll approval. Questions about an individual's pay go to Payroll at `payroll@example.invalid`; managers do not receive another employee's payslip through the normal leave-approval workflow.

## Policy questions

People Operations keeps the current handbook and records changes. This fictional handbook is for software testing and does not establish real employment terms.
