# ExampleCo New Starter Checklist

Effective date: 1 August 2026. Owner: People Operations. This is a fictional test document.

| When | Task | Responsible team | Sample status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before day one | Send start-time and location details | People Operations | Complete |
| Day one | Confirm account access and device handover | IT | Complete |
| Day one | Explain handbook, security reporting, and leave requests | People Operations | Complete |
| First week | Review role goals and team routines | Manager | In progress |
| First week | Confirm payroll profile in the private HR portal | Employee and Payroll | Pending |
| First month | Discuss training needs and feedback | Manager | Pending |

The employee enters their own payment details in the private portal. The checklist records only whether the payroll profile was confirmed; it never contains a bank account or identification number. People Operations keeps completion dates in the portal. All names and statuses in this sample are illustrative.
