# ExampleCo Manager Leave Approval Guide

Effective date: 1 August 2026. Owner: People Operations. This is a fictional test document.

1. Open the employee's leave request in the HR portal and confirm the dates and the displayed leave balance.
2. Check deadlines, planned team absences, and a reasonable coverage plan. Do not ask for a diagnosis in a shared calendar or team chat.
3. Approve or decline in the portal within three working days. For a decline, record a specific coverage reason and suggest another date if possible.
4. If the request involves a sensitive accommodation or a dispute, refer it to People Operations without copying medical details into the manager note.
5. Confirm that an approved request appears on the team calendar with only the dates and availability status.

The employee-facing allowance, notice, and correction rules are in the [leave and attendance policy](leave_and_attendance_policy.md). Approval of leave does not grant access to payroll, tax, compensation, or medical records. This guide is a fictional access-control fixture.
