# ExampleCo Benefits Enrolment Guide

Effective date: 1 August 2026. Owner: People Operations. This is a fictional test document.

## Open enrolment

The sample enrolment window runs from 1 to 15 September 2026. Employees review available plans in the private HR portal and submit their election by 17:00 Hong Kong time on 15 September. The portal sends a confirmation to the employee. Changes after the window require a qualifying event recorded with People Operations.

## Optional payroll deduction

The sample wellness plan costs an employee HKD 630 per month. The sample commuter plan costs HKD 400 per month. These values are illustrative; an individual's payslip shows the actual election and deduction for that person. Payroll reconciles plan elections with approved deductions before releasing pay. Employees ask Payroll about a deduction they do not recognize.

## Privacy

An enrolment question can be sent to `benefits@example.invalid`. Employees should use the portal to provide personal or health details. Managers see only scheduling information needed for work and do not receive individual benefit elections through this guide.
