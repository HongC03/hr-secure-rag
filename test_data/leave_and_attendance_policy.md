# ExampleCo Leave and Attendance Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026. Owner: People Operations. This is a fictional test document.

## Planned leave

ExampleCo's sample annual leave allowance is 18 working days per calendar year for a full-time employee. Employees request planned leave in the HR portal at least five working days before the first day away when practical. The manager checks team coverage and responds in the portal within three working days. An approval appears in the portal and reduces the employee's displayed leave balance.

## Unexpected absence

An employee who cannot work unexpectedly contacts their manager before their normal start time when practical and records the absence in the HR portal when able. They may give a private health detail directly to People Operations instead of placing it in a shared team channel. The manager records coverage needs, not a diagnosis. People Operations contacts the employee about any supporting documentation that applies to their situation.

## Late arrival and corrections

An employee tells their manager about a delay as soon as possible. A late arrival is recorded accurately; a manager can agree on a schedule adjustment. To correct a time entry, the employee submits a portal correction before the monthly payroll cutoff. Payroll uses only approved time and leave entries for the close run.

## Review and escalation

If a request is declined, the manager records the coverage reason. The employee can ask People Operations for a review. The [manager approval guide](manager_leave_approval_guide.md) gives managers an example workflow. All allowances and deadlines above are invented for this fixture.
